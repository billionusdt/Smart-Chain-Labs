Frequently Asked Questions

What types of MLM software does Smart Chain Labs develop?

We develop all major MLM compensation plan types — Binary MLM, Matrix MLM, Unilevel MLM, ROI MLM, Stair-Step MLM, Hybrid MLM and custom blockchain-based MLM platforms. All plans can be built with or without smart contract automation.

Which blockchain networks do you support?

We build smart contracts and DApps on BNB Chain (BSC), Polygon, Ethereum, and TRON. We also support USDT, USDC and other stablecoin integrations for seamless payment flows within MLM platforms.

How long does it take to build an MLM platform?

Project timelines depend on the complexity. A standard centralized MLM platform typically takes 3–6 weeks. A blockchain-based MLM DApp with smart contracts takes 4–10 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during the free consultation.

Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes. We provide 24/7 support, bug fixes, smart contract monitoring and platform upgrades after launch. Our team is available via WhatsApp and Telegram for immediate assistance.

Is Smart Chain Labs based in Hyderabad?

Yes. Our headquarters is in Hyderabad Kompally, Telangana, India. We also have offices in Bangalore, Dubai and Singapore. We serve clients pan-India and globally.
